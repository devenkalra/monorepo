                                            data-start="550" data-end="568">Experience Curve</em>, a concept based on

                                        activities...whether it...s travel, physical adventures, or social engagements...may

                                        diminish as our physical health and energy levels decrease with age. Perkins

                                                data-start="1613" data-end="1634">time-value of life.</em> The basic
