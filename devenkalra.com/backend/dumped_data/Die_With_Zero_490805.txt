                                        time is finite and cannot be replenished. The focus on time is central to his

                                        chapter is the <em data-start="1195" data-end="1210">"Time Bucket"</em>

                                        financial status, and personal priorities. These stages represent the best times

                                        allocate resources for those experiences.</p>

                                        most fulfilling activities at each stage of life.</p>
