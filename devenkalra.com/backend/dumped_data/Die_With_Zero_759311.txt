                                        philosophy behind the entire book: <strong data-start="672" data-end="787">spend

                                            your money wisely throughout your life, especially on experiences, so that

                                            you die with zero dollars left</strong>. He acknowledges that many people

                                        accumulate wealth without ever fully enjoying it, often for fear of running out

                                        of money in old age. The goal, according to Perkins, is to <strong

                                                data-start="961" data-end="1013">align your spending with your life...s

                                            experiences</strong>, ensuring that you use your wealth for its true

                                        purpose...enabling you to live fully and make memories that will last.</p>

                                    <p data-start="1132" data-e....nd="1445">By spending money during your life in a

                                        thoughtful way, you avoid leaving large sums behind at the end of your life.

                                        spending your money while you...re alive.</p><h4 data-start="1447"
